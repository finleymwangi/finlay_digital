How it works

From first conversation
to live website

A simple, transparent process — so you always know exactly where your project stands.

1

Discovery call

We start with a free conversation to understand your business, your goals, and what you need from your website.

2

Design & planning

We map out the structure, look, and feel of your site. You'll see exactly what it will look like before a single line of code is written.

3

Build & review

We build your site and walk you through it at every stage. Your feedback shapes the final result — no surprises at the end.

4

Launch & handover

We launch your site, hand over full ownership, and make sure you're confident managing it. We're still here if you need us after.

You have the right to:

7. How to contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please get in touch at finlaydigital0@gmail.com.
